# HONE raw data: live window and paid archive access (design, not built)

Status: design only, 2026-11-10. Requested by Josh. The live window is OFF, so all raw data is public.

## What exists today

- Devices upload one change-based raw blob per 15-minute window: `hone.raw` v1 records,
  gzip JSONL, with the schema registry served at `GET honemesh.net/api/raw/schema`.
- The blobs are public analytic content. Personal identifiers are tokens: BSSIDs and SSIDs are
  hashed with the res-7 area and the ISO week, BLE MACs with a per-device daily salt, and LoRa
  DevAddrs with a daily key. Locations are coarse H3 areas. Human speech is never recorded.
- The ingest (`/opt/hone-raw` on ferryvps, `honemesh.net/api/raw/*`) checks the seat-key signature,
  stores the blob and indexes it by (owner, device, window, cell, sha256).
- The blob's sha256 is the window's `SENSOR_SUMMARY.batch_hash`, so the chain commits to the exact
  bytes.
- `HONE_RAW_LIVE_WINDOW_S` is the switch, and it is `0` (off). When it is above 0, the reader
  endpoints answer 402 for windows older than the live window. That is the hook for this design.

## Goal

- Data younger than the live window W stays readable by anyone, so the network grows fast.
- Data older than W is held encrypted. Anyone can read it after paying on chain, and the
  payment goes to the people who keep the data (storage operators) and to the device owners.
- Nobody alone, including Honemesh, can decrypt the archive or censor a paid request.

## Network parameter

- Add `RAW_LIVE_WINDOW_EPOCHS` as a chain parameter. 0 means off. Governance changes it like
  other parameters, and it is announced N epochs ahead.
- Ingest and readers take it from the chain, not from local config.

## Archive encryption

1. When a window ages past W, an archiver encrypts the blob. It uses AES-256-GCM (or
   XChaCha20-Poly1305) with a fresh content key K_w for that blob.
2. K_w is secret-shared to an archive committee, t-of-n. The committee is the bonded storage
   and clock operators of the current committee epoch. Each operator gets its share
   encrypted to its X25519 key. The ciphertext, the shares and a commitment to K_w are stored.
   The archiver then deletes the plaintext and K_w.
3. An `ARCHIVE_SEAL` entry goes on chain:
   `{window, owner, device, blob_sha256 (plaintext, already in batch_hash), ct_sha256, committee_epoch, k_commitment}`.
   This links the encrypted copy to the plaintext hash the device committed.

A threshold-encryption variant needs no dealer: encrypt K_w to the committee's distributed
public key, generated by DKG once per committee epoch. Keys rotate per committee epoch. A share
handover re-shares to the next committee before an operator leaves.

## Paid read request

1. The reader submits `ARCHIVE_READ_REQUEST {range: (cell | device | owner, from_window, to_window), reader_pubkey, price, fee}`.
2. The chain escrows the payment. The price comes from the size in the index times a per-byte
   rate, and it is a chain parameter.
3. Each committee member checks the request is committed and paid. It then posts
   `ARCHIVE_KEY_SHARE {request_id, window, share encrypted to reader_pubkey, proof}`. The proof is
   a DLEQ / verifiable-decryption proof that the share matches the committed public share.
4. Once t valid shares are on chain, the escrow pays out:
   - x% to the share-releasing operators;
   - y% to the storage holders that served the ciphertext (proof of serving: the reader
     acknowledges, or the storage proofs Hone already runs);
   - z% to the device owner whose data it is.
5. The reader combines the shares to get K_w, fetches the ciphertext from any holder, checks
   `sha256(plaintext) == batch_hash` on chain, and decrypts.
6. If t shares are not posted within a timeout, the escrow refunds. An operator who misses
   requests loses reputation and, later, stake.

## Privacy rules that do not change

- The archive holds the same public content as the live tier, never more. There is no BSSID,
  SSID or MAC in the clear, and no human speech.
- Paying buys timely access to old analytic data, not identities.
- The owner can always read their own data for free through a self-request signed by the seat
  key, with a zero price.

## Chain entries needed later (for the chain agent)

- `RAW_LIVE_WINDOW_EPOCHS` parameter, 0 = off.
- `ARCHIVE_SEAL`, `ARCHIVE_READ_REQUEST`, `ARCHIVE_KEY_SHARE`, and escrow settlement.
- Committee key registry: X25519 keys per operator per committee epoch, or the DKG transcript.

## Related chain items from the same build (needed now, behind gates)

- `SENSOR_SUMMARY` with an empty or null cell, for readings where every location source failed.
  Today the app keeps those rows queued and sends them once the gate exists. Their raw blobs
  already reach the ingest.
- `RAW_SCHEMA_ANCHOR {registry: "hone.raw", version, sha256}`, signed by the network or
  shindevlin key. It anchors the schema-registry hash, so readers can prove which schema a
  record follows. The current registry sha256 is shown by `GET /api/raw/health`.
