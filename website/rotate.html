BTCPC Owner Key Rotation

Runs entirely in your browser — your private key never leaves this device.

Rotate Owner Key

This is the nuclear option. Only do this if your owner key was compromised or you are migrating to a new key. Active / posting / memo key rotation can be done through the Telegram bot with your password.
The private key is used locally to sign the rotation payload. Only your new public key and a cryptographic signature are sent to the server. The server never sees your private key.
