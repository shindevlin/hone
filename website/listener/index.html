Questions

Does it record what we say at home?

No. Sound is checked a few seconds at a time, on your own computer. Anything that sounds like people talking is deleted right away and only counted. No audio is ever saved or sent. Every 15 minutes the listener sends a short report: which birds it heard, and when.

Which cameras work?

Most home security and IP cameras with a built-in microphone, on the same network as your computer. The installer looks for them and shows you the ones it can hear.

Do I need a computer that's always on?

The listener only hears birds while the computer is on. A Raspberry Pi 5 is a cheap, silent way to listen all day and night; see the Linux and Raspberry Pi card above.

I only have an iPhone.

That's fine. The listener runs on a computer at home and your iPhone shows your birds at honemesh.net/me. Add it to your home screen for one-tap access.

Will it slow down my computer?

It's built to run quietly in the background. You can pause or uninstall it at any time.
