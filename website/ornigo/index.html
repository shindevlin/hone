Questions

Does it record what we say at home?

No. Sound is checked a few seconds at a time on your own computer. Anything that sounds like people talking is deleted right away and only counted. No audio is ever saved or sent. Every 15 minutes Ornigo sends a short report: which birds it heard, and when.

Which cameras work?

Most Wi-Fi security cameras with a microphone. Ornigo finds them on your network and tells you in plain words how well each one hears birds.

Will it slow my computer down?

It runs quietly in the background on a small slice of the processor. You can pause or uninstall it at any time.
