01 — Why This Exists

The internet runs on rented ground.

  • Your AI runs on someone else's cloud. They set the price, the rules, the rate limits.
  • Your code lives on someone else's platform. They can ban your account or shut down the service.
  • Your store depends on someone else's marketplace. They take 15% and can delist you without appeal.
  • Your sensors create data someone else monetizes. You get nothing.
  • GPUs burn electricity proving effort to no one — 150 TWh/year on hashes that produce nothing useful.

Bitcoin proved that money could run without a company. HONE asks the next question: can compute, storage, sensors, commerce, and code hosting run the same way?

The answer is yes — if the mining is the work itself.

06 — Security

Protocol-level multi-factor authentication

HONE enforces a four-key hierarchy at the protocol level. A compromised mining machine cannot reach your funds.

Four-Key Hierarchy

Four key pairs from a single BIP-39 mnemonic: Owner (cold storage), Active (financial), Posting (mining operations), Memo (communication). Keys are scoped — your GPU rig only ever holds the posting key.

Protocol-Enforced 2FA

Password-derived key signatures enforced at the consensus level. Configurable per account. Not wallet-level security — the network itself rejects unauthenticated high-value operations.

72-Hour Recovery

Submit a recovery request with your Owner key. A 72-hour time-lock begins on-chain. If the real owner contests during that window, the recovery is blocked. After 72 hours, 2FA resets safely.
