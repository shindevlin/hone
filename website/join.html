Questions

Which cameras work?

Most Wi-Fi security cameras with a microphone. Ornigo tells you in plain words how well each one hears birds.

I only have an iPhone.

Install Ornigo on a computer that stays on, then add honemesh.net/me to your Home Screen.

Will it slow my computer down?

It runs quietly in the background on a small slice of the processor. You can pause or uninstall it at any time.
