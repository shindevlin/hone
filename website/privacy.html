HONE Privacy Policy

This policy explains what HONE may collect when you use the website, app, controller flow, or network, and how the data is used.

1. Data we may collect

  • Account identifiers, usernames, and wallet addresses you choose to link.
  • Device and browser information needed to run the app and diagnose issues.
  • Network and usage logs such as page requests, node status, and inference usage.
  • Content you submit for inference, support, or transfer authorization.
  • Controller and policy metadata when you opt into spending controls.

2. Local wallet data

HONE may create or import a wallet export during setup. The export is designed to be saved to your device. HONE does not want you to expose your private keys; you are responsible for securing any export file, mnemonic phrase, or controller credential you keep locally.

3. Controller and external providers

The controller flow may use Privy or other supported wallet providers to sign approvals. Those providers may process login, wallet, and signing-related data under their own policies. If you use Telegram, GitHub, RPC providers, or other third-party integrations, their policies may also apply.

4. How data is used

  • Run HONE services and authenticate you.
  • Process wallet actions, inference requests, and node participation.
  • Maintain chain state, logs, rewards, and policy enforcement.
  • Improve reliability, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures.

5. Data retention

HONE keeps data only as long as needed for network operation, security, accounting, legal obligations, or user-requested features. On-chain records may persist as part of the blockchain and cannot be deleted retroactively.

6. Your choices

You can choose which wallets to link, whether to use controller mode, and whether to use browser, desktop, or terminal workflows. If a feature depends on a third-party provider, you can stop using that provider at any time.

7. Changes

HONE may update this policy as the product evolves. Review the current version periodically.
