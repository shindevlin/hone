Mine clock rewards from your Android phone

Tap your browser menu → "Add to Home Screen" to keep this page handy.
1Install Termux
Get Termux from F-Droid. Don't use the Play Store version — it's outdated and broken.
Install Termux from F-Droid
2Tap to Copy the Command
This copies the install command to your clipboard. Then switch to Termux and long-press to paste.
curl https://honemesh.net/android.sh | bash
3Switch to Termux & Paste
Open the Termux app (from your apps drawer or recent apps). Long-press inside Termux → tap "Paste" → press Enter. It will ask for your HONE username. If you don't have one, message @btcpcbot on Telegram first.
Done
Your clock node runs in the background, auto-updates every hour, and survives reboots. View your stats at honemesh.net/clock.
