Mine clock rewards from your Android phone

Tap your browser menu → "Add to Home Screen" to keep this page handy.
1Install Termux
Get Termux from F-Droid (official source). Don't use the Play Store version — it's outdated.
Install Termux from F-Droid
2Open Termux & Paste
Open the Termux app and paste this command. It installs Node, downloads the BTCPC clock node, and starts mining.
curl https://btcpc.net/android.sh | bash
Open Termux
3Enter Your Username
When prompted, enter your BTCPC username. Don't have one? Message @btcpcbot on Telegram first to create an account.
That's it
The clock node runs in the background, auto-updates every hour, and survives reboots. View your stats at btcpc.net/clock or in the BTCPC dashboard.
