# HONE Improvement Scan — 2026-07-06

**Inaugural full-stack sweep.** Six parallel research agents swept GitHub across HONE's
major subsystems. Every star count, license, and maintenance date below was verified
against the live GitHub repo in July 2026 (agents were instructed not to answer from
memory). Verdicts: **DEP** = adopt as dependency · **FORK** = fork & adapt · **STUDY** =
study the idea, rebuild/keep-out (license, language, or design mismatch).

---

## Executive summary — the 8 highest-leverage findings

Ranked by objective impact on HONE (correctness first, then sovereignty, then cost-to-maintain):

| # | Change | Subsystem | Verdict | Why it's #-ranked |
|---|--------|-----------|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | **wasmtime JIT → `wasmi` interpreter** | Consensus | DEP | Removes a *real, latent consensus-divergence bug*. A JIT is non-deterministic across platforms/versions; every node must reach byte-identical contract results. wasmi is a deterministic interpreter, audited (SRLabs + Runtime Verification), with native fuel metering (replaces your bolted-on gas). **Single highest-value change in this scan.** |
| 2 | **Founder sign-request policy → FROST 2-of-3** | Identity | DEP | Turns the "never auto-sign; route to shindevlin/natoshisakamoto/josh" *policy* into a *cryptographic* threshold guarantee. Produces an **ordinary ed25519 signature** — zero `tx.rs`/consensus changes, composes with existing `ed25519-dalek 2` verifier. Audited (NCC). |
| 3 | **qwen "multi-arch" is a wiring bug, not a candle gap** | Inference | DEP+STUDY | `candle-transformers` **already ships** `qwen2`/`qwen3`/`qwen2_moe`/`qwen3_moe` + quantized. The #1 pain is a missing config-driven dispatch registry in your `inference` module — not a candle limitation. Study `Crane`/`mistral.rs` for the registry pattern (both pure-candle, MIT, mandate-compatible). |
| 4 | **Storage trait + `redb`/`fjall` for mobile** | Storage | DEP | RocksDB's C++ toolchain is the friction behind painful Android/Flipper/aarch64 builds — the tax on "phone is a full node." Put state behind a `StateStore` trait; ship pure-Rust `redb` (read-opt) or `fjall` (LSM, RocksDB-like) on mobile, keep RocksDB on servers. **Surfaced independently by 3 of 6 agents.** |
| 5 | **Proof-of-inference = determinism + fraud proofs, NOT zkML** | Inference | STUDY | zkML (EZKL) can't prove LLM-scale work on phones today (~18M-param ceiling vs 0.6B+ models). The viable design is **optimistic re-execution + fraud-proof dispute game** (OPML pattern), *gated on* batch-invariant deterministic kernels (Thinking Machines spec). Without batch-invariance, honest miners disagree and consensus breaks. |
| 6 | **Custom VRF → `schnorrkel` or `fastcrypto` ECVRF** | Consensus | DEP | Replaces hand-rolled VRF with field-proven audited crypto (schnorrkel powers Polkadot BABE). This is the *sanctioned* direction — replacing homegrown crypto with audited, not the reverse. |
| 7 | **`iroh` as an additional transport + `pkarr` discovery** | Networking | DEP | Purpose-built QUIC NAT-traversal with relay fallback — materially better hole-punch success than libp2p DCUtR on hostile/mobile networks, with Swift/Kotlin FFI. Add *alongside* libp2p (keep gossipsub); self-host relays for sovereignty. `pkarr` gives serverless signed-key discovery over Mainline DHT. |
| 8 | **`turmoil`/`madsim` deterministic simulation testing** | Node infra | FORK/STUDY | Directly targets the "zero-peers must not apply / no-fork-when-disconnected" hardline. Lets you deterministically reproduce a partition/crash that would fork the chain. Highest *confidence* payoff; requires building a sim seam around consensus. |

**Cross-cutting decision that gates several items:** adopting real CIDs (storage #2 below)
forces a **one-time content-addressing choice — sha256-CID vs BLAKE3-CID**. It touches
genesis-adjacent format surface, so decide it *once, early, pre-mainnet*. Per HONE's own
launch history, late format churn is exactly what breaks launches.

## Convergence signals (independent agents agreeing = strong)

- **redb/fjall** surfaced from storage, consensus, *and* inference agents → the mobile
  storage-trait refactor is the most-corroborated structural win.
- **iroh** surfaced from both networking and storage → QUIC/NAT-traversal + verified blobs
  are one coherent adoption, not two.
- **"replace hand-rolled crypto with audited crates, never the reverse"** was reached
  independently by the consensus (VRF→schnorrkel) and identity (FROST, k256) agents →
  consistent with the project's stated crypto rule.

## Recommended adoption sequence (objective, dependency-ordered)

1. **`wasmi`** swap — retire the JIT determinism risk. *(correctness; self-contained)*
2. **Storage trait + redb/fjall bench** — unblock clean mobile builds. *(corroborated x3)*
3. **candle dispatch registry** for qwen — study Crane, no new dep. *(unblocks the #1 pain)*
4. **Decide sha256-CID vs BLAKE3-CID**, then adopt `ipld-core`. *(pre-mainnet format gate)*
5. **FROST founder vault** — policy → cryptographic guarantee. *(security)*
6. **schnorrkel/fastcrypto VRF** swap. *(security)*
7. **iroh transport + pkarr discovery** (self-hosted relays). *(sovereignty/reachability)*
8. **utoipa + metrics + tokio-console** — operator visibility & client contract. *(cheap, high-ROI)*
9. **turmoil sim seam** — chain-fork confidence for the no-peers hardline. *(deepest, do deliberately)*

Proof-of-inference (batch-invariant determinism + OPML fraud-proof pattern) is a
**design-track**, not a swap — sequence it as its own spec once #1–#3 land.
